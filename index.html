The Adaptation Engine

No More Meltdowns.
Only Breakthroughs.

A furrowed brow. Eyes drifting to the window. A third wrong answer in a row. Chiron reads the moment your child is about to check out — and changes the entire lesson before frustration turns into a fight.

  1. 01

    It notices

    The camera quietly reads facial signals — brow furrows, distraction, that distinctive “I hate this” expression — alongside answer speed and accuracy.

  2. 02

    It waits for the beat

    Chiron never interrupts mid-thought. It lets your child finish the question they're on, then makes its move at a natural seam in the lesson.

  3. 03

    It switches the modality

    The quiz becomes a 3D block quest. Or a Socratic voice chat. Or a stretch break. Same concept, completely different doorway in.
