Questions parents ask

When can we actually start using Chiron? +

Chiron is in closed beta right now, and we haven't announced a public release date. We're adding families in small batches so we can watch how the adaptation engine performs for each child. Join the waitlist and we'll email you when a spot opens.

How does the 3-month free trial work? +

Every family starts with three full months of Chiron at no cost — the complete product, not a limited version. Billing only begins after those three months, at whichever plan you chose.

What's the difference between monthly and yearly? +

Only the billing cycle and the price. Monthly is $10, which comes to $120 over a year. Yearly is $80 up front, saving you $40 — about $6.67 a month. Both plans unlock exactly the same features.

Do I need a credit card to join the waitlist? +

No. Joining the waitlist takes an email address and your child's grade, nothing more. We'll only contact you about Chiron.

How does the camera work, and can I turn it off? +

Chiron's affect detection uses the camera to read engagement signals and adjust the lesson in the moment. Camera access is controlled by you from the Parent Portal and can be switched off at any time.
